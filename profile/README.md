## Products

### POLYDESK clicktrader

[![Latest release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/itereon-dev/polydesk-clicktrader-releases?label=release&sort=semver)](https://github.com/itereon-dev/polydesk-clicktrader-releases/releases/latest)
[![Windows · macOS](https://img.shields.io/badge/platforms-Windows%20%7C%20macOS-1f2937)](https://github.com/itereon-dev/polydesk-clicktrader-releases#download)
[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/web-tradepolydesk.com-0a7cff)](https://www.tradepolydesk.com)

**The desktop app for ladder trading on Polymarket.** A one-click price ladder in the
style of exchange trading tools, with quick take, quick cancel, stake presets, hedging
and cash-out figures, position and P&L tracking. Built for traders who want to click on a
price, not fill in a form.

- **Non-custodial.** Runs on your computer; your keys and funds never leave your control. Nobody places orders on your behalf.
- **Hard limits and kill switch.** Stake, open-order and exposure limits enforced by the engine; *CANCEL ALL* pulls every working order.
- **Paper first.** Paper mode is the default and simulates fills against the real market.
- **Windows 10/11 (x64) and macOS (Apple Silicon).** Signed, verified in-app updates.
- **Open beta.** Free tier with live trading included; Plus subscription lifts the size caps.
